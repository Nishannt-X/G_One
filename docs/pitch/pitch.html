G-One

The voice in the middle,
on the farmer's side.

A voice assistant that brings a buyer onto the farmer's call, translates both ways, and quietly tells the farmer what the buyer can't hear.

Built on Gnani Prisma + Gnani Timbre One-page solution pitch Great Indian AI Internship Challenge Team of two

The problem

Most Indian farmers sell small lots, by phone, to buyers they cannot see, often across a language gap. Whoever translates on that call usually works for the other side.

The farmer hears "the market is down" with no reference point, and payment scams ("scan this QR and enter your PIN") target exactly these calls. The phone is already in their hand; an app and English are not.

The solution

G-One sits on the call and works for the farmer. The farmer speaks their own language. G-One finds a matching buyer, asks the buyer's permission, brings them on, and stays in the middle.

  • Translates both ways: Kannada farmer ⇄ Bengali buyer, each in their own language.
  • Cautions against traps: a PIN or OTP request gets a fixed caution, never repeating the secret.
  • Two spoken yeses: terms read back to each side, then a bilingual Sauda Parchi (a record, not a contract).
  • Remembers, with consent: greets returning farmers by name; "मुझे भूल जाओ" forgets them.

Gnani at the core

PrismaSpeech to text for both parties: streaming on 8 kHz PCM16, with per-turn upload as a fall back.

Timbre v2.5G-One's voice in every language. Numbers are spelled out as words so they sound natural.

Keys stay on the backend. An OpenAI-compatible LLM with tools (mandi price, weather, buyer match, price fairness, scam patterns) is the brain; Sarvam translates.

What makes it different

  • Separate audio per party, so private whispers are possible at all.
  • Built for conversation: barge-in that ignores a short "haan", patience after "aur… / lekin…", echo checks, LLM failover within 1.5 s.
  • Honest by design: dated price references, never "today's price"; memory keyed by an HMAC of the number; IDs-only logs; synthetic data only.
